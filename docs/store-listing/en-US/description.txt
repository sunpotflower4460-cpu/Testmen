Add the things you want to keep doing, and focus on one question: did you do it today?
On the days you did, press a stamp to record it. A simple, satisfying habit tracker.

■ One tap, no fuss
Each habit is either done or not yet. When it’s done, just press the big stamp. Keeping a record takes almost no effort.

■ A stamp that’s fun to press
A springy landing, splashes of ink, and flying emoji. Every press gives you a small sense of achievement.

■ A calendar of dates × habits
Dates run down the side and habits across the top, so you can see at a glance what you kept up and when. Streaks and monthly totals are counted for you.

■ Make it yours
• Add as many habits as you like
• Pick a stamp icon (emoji) and an ink color
• Reorder, edit, and delete freely
• Turn sound effects on or off
• Back up your data (export / import)

■ About ads
A banner ad appears at the bottom of the screen. The one-time “Remove ads” purchase (in Settings) hides it. There are no other charges.

Your records are saved on your device, and no account is needed. Start today.
