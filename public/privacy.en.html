Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 / App name: Stamp Habit

This Privacy Policy explains how user information is handled in the mobile app “Stamp Habit” (the “App”).

1. Information collected and stored

  • Habit records: the habits you add, your daily records, and your settings. These are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer’s servers.
  • Account information: the App does not require an account and does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address (except when you contact us).

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements from Google AdMob (a banner at the bottom of the screen). To serve ads, AdMob may collect and use certain data, such as advertising identifiers (including the IDFA), approximate location, and device information. For details, see Google’s advertising policies.

In regions where it is required, a consent form is shown before ads are loaded. On iOS, once ads can be requested, a dialog asking for permission to track is shown. If you do not allow tracking, general ads are shown. If a “Remove ads” purchase is confirmed at launch, ads are not initialized, requested, or displayed at all.

3. In-app purchases

The “Remove ads” purchase is processed through Apple’s App Store (StoreKit). Your purchase status is managed on your device and by Apple’s systems. The developer does not obtain purchase information or payment details such as credit card numbers.

4. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell or provide user information to third parties, except as required by law. The advertising provider (Google) and Apple handle information according to their own policies.

5. Deleting your data

You can erase the records on your device at any time by deleting individual habits or by deleting the App.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from any particular age group.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as necessary. Important changes will be announced in the App or by other means.
