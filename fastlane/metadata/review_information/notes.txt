No login or account is required. All features are available immediately.

Purpose and target audience:
"Stamp Habit" is a simple habit tracker for anyone who wants to build daily habits (e.g., reading, exercise, drinking water). It focuses on one question — "Did I do it today?" — and lets users record it with a single tap of a Japanese-style stamp (hanko). A calendar matrix (dates x habits) and streak counts show progress at a glance.

How to use:
- Tap "+" at the bottom to create a habit (title, stamp emoji, ink color).
- On the Today tab, tap the large stamp to mark the habit as done today; tap again to undo.
- On the Calendar tab, view records by date and tap past cells to add or remove records.
- Tap "..." on a habit card to edit, reorder, or delete it.
- Tap the gear icon for settings: remove ads (in-app purchase), restore purchases, sound effects, data export/import (JSON backup), terms of use, and privacy policy.

Ads and in-app purchase (new in 1.1):
- A banner ad (Google AdMob) is shown at the bottom of the screen. The App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown before personalized ads are requested; ads are still shown (non-personalized) if the user declines.
- "Remove Ads" (Product ID: remove_ads) is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase that permanently hides all ads. It can be purchased from Settings (gear icon > "広告を消す（プレミアム）") and restored via "購入を復元" in Settings.
- Purchases are processed by Apple and managed with RevenueCat.

External services:
Google AdMob (advertising), RevenueCat (in-app purchase management). Habit data is stored locally on the device and never sent to any server.

Regional differences:
The app functions identically in all regions. Currently distributed in Japan only; the UI is in Japanese.
